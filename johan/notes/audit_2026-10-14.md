## Findings that change the notes

1. **D9 (median age at death) is probably not a real discrepancy.** The notes compare the
   paper's 64 with the pooled 1990-2015 median (58). The yearly medians in our data run
   49 (1990) ... 64 (2012-2013) ... 65 (2015). The paper's 64 sits within one year of the
   2015 value, so the paper most likely reports a recent year. Before the write-up says
   "unexplained", test that reading. Cumulative share at age 64 in 2015 is 48.6%, at age 65
   it is 50.5%, so a one-year convention difference flips it.
2. **D4 carries two different 2015 values.** The notes quote 4.34 (benchmark), while
   `targets.csv` and this audit give 4.39 (our pipeline). The two should agree if the
   pipelines are identical to 3e-7. Re-check once Stata is available, then use one number.
3. **Botswana (K3/K4) is end-year sensitive.** 1990 to 2000: HC -12.5%, GD -1.1%. 1990 to
   2001: HC -11.4%, GD +0.5%. The paper's pair (-12, +0.7) matches neither. Report the
   sensitivity, not one pair.
4. **K6 is untracked.** Botswana HC in 2000 is 29.43 (paper 29.5). Add it to D11, or treat
   it as the same PovCalNet-vintage issue.
5. **Several "PASS" rows pass only after re-rounding.** T3a, T3e, T3f, T3i-T3l, T3n and
   T4a, T4d pass against `ours_pr`, the paper's hand-rounding applied to our numbers. That is
   defensible (D14), but the write-up should say so, not claim a direct match. Direct,
   unrounded matches are the PASS rows with an empty `ours_pr`.
6. **"39 of 51 reproduced" is a tolerance count.** Tolerances range from 0 to 5 (living
   population is allowed 5 m). Quote it together with the tolerance column.

## Stata re-run (2026-10-15, Windows, Stata 17 SE)

**Our pipeline.** `scripts/01`-`07`, `09`, `10` re-run from the raw package (`08` skipped: no
HMD files in `data/raw/hmd`). Every `.dta` in `data/clean` and every table in
`output/replication/tables` is **identical to the committed version** (max relative
difference 0). The pipeline is reproducible across macOS and Windows. `hmd_france.dta` and
`compare_benchmark.csv` were not regenerated.

**Authors' code.** Run in a scratch copy of the package (not in `data/raw`) with only these
edits: `Base` path, `run` -> `do`, `br` -> `list`, HMD step and Figure 5 block commented out.
It runs to the end and writes `PovertyMortalityWorld.dta`.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Our GD vs the authors' output, all 113 countries x 26 years (sorted by year, name-free) | Max gap 1.5e-5 percentage points. World: 6e-6 |
| Gabon, Morocco 1993 | 18.709 and 15.661, as ours |
| Average re-rank, all years / 1990 / 2015 | **4.136 / 4.920 / 4.336**. Ours: 4.138 / 4.920 / 4.389 |
| Azerbaijan ranks lost | 26 in 2008, 25 in 2014; overall max 26 (2008) |
| Table 3 `di` lines | 52.79% and 17.23%, life-span share 27.13 / 36.33, 60.35 at a-hat = 80 |
| Reversals, the authors' filtered calculation (non-missing country ratios, 2,340 country-years) | **8.419%** at a-hat = 50 (paper: 8.4), **26.88%** as gamma -> infinity (paper: 26.9). 10.77% at a-hat = 80 |

What this settles:

- **D1, D3, D4, D10, D12 now rest on a run on this machine**, not only on the earlier macOS
  run. The notes' benchmark numbers (4.34, 4.92, Azerbaijan 26 in 2008) reproduce exactly.
- **Finding 2 above is resolved.** 4.34 is the authors' code, 4.39 is our pipeline. They
  differ only in 2015 (1990 agrees to 6 digits), where 4 countries have HC = 0 and 10 have
  HC < 0.1%, so ranks among them are tie-dependent. In the write-up quote the authors'
  4.92 and 4.34, say that our tie rule gives 4.39, and note that either way the paper's
  4.4 and 4.9 are swapped.
- **Reversals match the paper and our `targets.csv`.** The authors' own filtered code gives
  8.419% and 26.880%. (The log also prints an unfiltered 7.69% from inside a loop. It
  includes missing and regional rows and is not the paper's statistic. Do not quote it.)

Still not verifiable here: D15 and D16 (Figure 5, HMD vintage). They need `lt_both.zip` and
`population.zip` in `data/raw/hmd/`, which are not in the clone and need an HMD login.
